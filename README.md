## A note from the desk

I'm a full-stack and mobile developer from Tangerang, Indonesia. I freelance, and most of my best work happens late at night with a pot of tea slowly going cold next to the keyboard. I like software that quietly makes someone's day easier, and I care about the small craft details around it: a well-named function, a tidy query, an interface with a bit of personality.

## Where I've been

I spent about a year as an apprentice at PT Tri Nindya Utama, building enterprise apps for government and corporate clients. That meant audit management systems for Kemenbud and Waskita Karya, plus e-TILA, SIGMA MRT and the e-Audit mobile app. It's where I got properly comfortable with Laravel, PostgreSQL tuning, code quality gates and everything else that only matters once real people depend on your work.

In 2025 I reached the national finals of LKI in the IT Software Solutions for Business category, representing Banten Province.

## On the workbench

Right now I'm building **Civic Nest**, a residential management system on React Router v7, alongside freelance work. I also keep a small shelf of side projects going: **Onyx Storage**, an equipment borrowing system on React and Laravel with Inertia.js, **Onyx Habit**, a habit tracker with a Bun and Elysia.js backend on Prisma, and a learning management system with a React Router v7 frontend and a Laravel API.

I'm currently getting deeper into Django and Flutter, and I'm always poking at new tools that make my workflow a little less clumsy.

## The toolkit

Laravel is home base, and React with TypeScript is where I spend most of my frontend hours, usually with Inertia.js or TanStack in the mix. For other backends I reach for Elysia.js, Express or NestJS. On mobile I move between Flutter and Kotlin Compose, with some Java left over from my early Android days.
